human.bin is built by tools/avatar/build_human.py from MakeHuman assets
(base mesh hm08, targets, default skeleton weights).

These assets were released by the MakeHuman team under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain dedication): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman (makehuman/data), http://www.makehumancommunity.org

Changes: neutral-ethnicity gender targets averaged; face cavities removed and holes filled;
data quantized into a compact binary format.
